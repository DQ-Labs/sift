Working now

Recursive folder indexing, search and filters, original-speed auditioning, persistent gems and sets, four-layer mixing, locks, mute, solo, levels and stereo WAV export.

Audio behavior

Tempo sync and session key use Rubber Band for the same processing in playback and export. Rerolls favor nearby tempos, smaller pitch shifts and known metadata. A session key matches major/minor scales with the smallest shift (up to six semitones); relative major/minor share a scale. This preserves chord character and does not guarantee matching chord progressions. Drums and unknown keys keep their pitch; unknown tempos keep their speed. Locks keep sample choices, while session settings still apply. Each layer is prepared as the same four-bar cycle in 4/4 for playback and export. Auto repeats short loops only when they fit a usable beat grid; other sounds play once per cycle with silence or trimming. Blank start offsets skip leading silence; layer labels show the chosen source time. The clock control sets an exact start by ear and overrides repeat length. No automatic downbeat detection is claimed. Waveforms show the prepared cycle, including repeats and silence.

Automatic analysis

Essentia estimates tempo and musical key locally, using up to the first 60 seconds. Short, quiet or ambiguous sounds may remain unknown. Explicit BPM/key filename labels take priority over audio estimates; disagreements are flagged for review. Sample details shows the evidence and lets you correct values; your corrections survive reanalysis. Sound types use filename roles, then the nearest informative folder within the indexed source.

The demo collection

Twelve original synthesized loops, all at 92 BPM. They are here to try the controls. Add your folder to discover your own recordings.

Large files

Indexing reads metadata without loading all audio. Workbench playback decodes only selected samples, with a bounded cache. Files over 100 MB can be auditioned but are excluded from the workbench. Tempo sync supports source clips up to 3 minutes and stretched output up to 6 minutes. Its local cache is capped at approximately 512 MB.
